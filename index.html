Operations Control Center

A planning and tracking view for an operations team. It answers three questions in one place: do we have enough people, how are we doing against plan, and what is automation taking off the team.

How it works

The dashboard combines four synthetic inputs into one view, so a manager can plan capacity, spot gaps and report impact without juggling separate spreadsheets:

  • Team & training data: trained and ramping headcount per team and queue, week by week. New hires ramp for 6 weeks at half capacity.
  • Demand plan: expected tasks per queue per week, with a mid-period peak.
  • Actuals: completed task volumes for weeks 1–18, compared with the plan.
  • Automation results: tasks each agent handled, converted to hours saved (minutes saved per task) and cost avoided (hours × 38 CU).

Every filter recalculates all three sections from the same records, so the numbers always agree with each other.
